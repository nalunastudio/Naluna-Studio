Cancellation & Refund Policy

Our songs and videos are personalised digital content, created specifically for you and delivered electronically as soon as they are ready. This affects your cancellation rights as described below.

1. Before you pay

You can leave the order process at any point before paying, at no cost and with no obligation. You can preview a free sample of your song before deciding whether to purchase.

2. After you pay — immediate start, and loss of the 14-day right to cancel

At checkout, you are asked to expressly confirm that you want us to begin creating your personalised song or video immediately. Because you give this consent, and because creation genuinely starts right away, you lose your statutory 14-day right to cancel once creation has started. This is in line with the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 (UK) and, where applicable, Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU (EU consumers).

This does not remove your other rights — see Section 3 below if something goes wrong with your order.

3. Faulty or non-conforming content

If any of the following applies:

  • your order fails to generate and cannot be completed;
  • the delivered song or video does not match what you ordered because of an error on our side (for example, the wrong recipient name, the wrong package, or a genuine technical fault);
  • the file you received is corrupted or does not play,

contact us at contact@nalunastudio.com within 14 days of delivery, with your order reference and a description of the problem. We will review your case and, as appropriate:

  • correct or regenerate the affected part of your order at no extra cost, or
  • issue a full or partial refund to your original payment method.

We do not operate a blanket "no refunds" policy — every request is reviewed individually, on its own facts.

4. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Stripe. Depending on your bank, this typically takes 5–10 business days to appear on your statement.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any right you have under UK or, where applicable, EU consumer protection law, including your rights regarding digital content that is faulty or not as described.

6. Contact

For any cancellation, refund, or order issue, contact contact@nalunastudio.com.
